## Overview
This repository provides the open-source computational evaluation script for the Connecting Thread Theory (TBP)[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span). The TBP framework proposes a 6-dimensional topological matrix modeled to evaluate fundamental physical constants[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span). Within the limits of this geometric model, parameters such as particle mass hierarchies and cosmological energy budgets are analytically derived, structurally minimizing reliance on empirical curve-fitting[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span). This script utilizes symbolic computation to verify the internal dimensional consistency and the mathematically singularity-free nature of the proposed framework[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).

## Explicit Assumptions and Limitations
The computations provided in this repository are strictly theoretical[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span). The primary assumption of this framework is the formalization of a quantized spatial capacity constrained by an absolute thermodynamic ceiling ($R_{crit} \approx 215.111$ GeV)[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span). The script provides evaluations up to first-order geometric friction and second-order kinematic backscattering limits[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span). Extrapolations beyond these evaluated parameters, particularly concerning higher-order quantum field fluctuations, remain speculative and are subject to future methodological development[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).

## Computational Stages
The codebase executes a linear algorithmic verification of the TBP mathematical architecture:

* **Fundamental Parameters & Electroweak Sector**: Evaluates matrix dimensionality and formally derives the theoretical Higgs scalar equilibrium ($\sim 125.22$ GeV)[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).
* **QCD & Cosmology**: Models the proton equilibrium ($\sim 938.26$ MeV) and calculates a theoretical base for the Dark Sector (evaluating Dark Energy at $\approx 68.67\%$)[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).
* **Fermion Spectrum**: Derives lepton and quark mass limits using deterministic topological friction operators[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).
* **Boundary Limits & Unitarity**: Evaluates high-energy asymptotic bounce limits and the fundamental $SU(2)$ isospin asymmetry ratio ($17/18$)[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span).
* **Extreme Conditions**: Models the potential isolation of structural topological defects when energy inputs exceed $R_{crit}$[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span).
* **Higher-Order Perturbations**: Computes 2-loop kinematic backscattering and the localized terrestrial observer bias ($\Sigma\Phi_{local}$)[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span).
